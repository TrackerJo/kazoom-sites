## Overall Impression

The fixes landed and a blind reviewer noticed them: the scroll-spy nav, the real start form with its exemplary inline validation and warm success state, the testimonial pause control, the screen-reader counter value, and the coral-deep AA panels were all independently called out as strengths. The page moved from 31 to 34. Two things now stand between it and a 36+: a trust-critical counter that can display "$0/mo," and a motion budget that a calm, skeptical, phone-first audience may read as flashy. Biggest single opportunity: make the counter impossible to strand at 0, then decide whether the "showy" branch is actually serving the visitor or performing for the portfolio.

## What's Working

1. **The MiniSite system is the product demo and the page's superpower.** Four bespoke example sites with distinct palettes and typefaces prove "not a template" better than any copy. Real craft (container queries, browser chrome, per-site tokens); no generator ships this.
2. **Committed-warm color executed with discipline, now with verified contrast.** Coral carries the surface, neutrals are hue-tinted toward it, and the new coral-deep panel bases mean the dimmed copy in Pricing and the FinalCTA actually clears AA. Someone reasoned about contrast rather than eyeballing it.
3. **Copy and error states speak the audience's language.** "If you can send a text message, you can do this." "That email looks off, mind checking it?" "No payment needed yet." The warm/trustworthy/capable voice landed in words, disarming the "burned by something complicated" fear.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The Stats strip can display "$0/mo" (verified in capture).**
- **Why it matters:** The visible counter text is hardcoded to `0` and only updates when `useInView` (`margin: '-15%'`) fires. In any pre-scroll, pre-JS, or IO-late state it shows "0 days / $0/mo / 0 days" (confirmed: it renders $0 even with the section inside the captured viewport). A literal $0 price is the single most damaging thing this page can flash at a pricing-anxious owner: it reads as broken or bait-and-switch at the exact proof moment. For normal slow scrolling it most likely counts up, but depending on IntersectionObserver to render *any* value is fragile. Treat as P0 if live scrolling does not reliably count it up.
- **Fix:** Render the final value as the default text so it is never stranded at 0; animate 0 to the value only when `inView && !reduce`. Widen or drop the `-15%` margin. The screen-reader value is already correct, so only the visible path needs this.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden` (or a direct fix).

**[P1] Motion budget still risks the "calm, never showy" line.**
- **Why it matters:** Even after the conservative trim, the page stacks aurora drift, hero parallax, 3D tilt, moving glare, magnetic buttons, word-by-word reveal, marquee, count-up, price sheen sweep, auto-rotating carousel, and per-step scrub. Individually tasteful; collectively a maximalist vocabulary on a brand whose north star is quiet confidence. For a skeptical, non-technical owner, over-animation reads adjacent to the loud-hard-sell anti-reference.
- **Fix:** Pick 2-3 signature moves (hero reveal, MiniSite tilt, scroll-progress) and demote the rest. Strongest candidates to cut: the magnetic buttons (gimmicky for this audience) and the price sheen sweep (reads promotional on an otherwise honest panel).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable quieter`.

**[P1] Steps sticky-pin still spends ~3 viewports on 3 short, near-identical cards.**
- **Why it matters:** Even at the reduced `clamp(420px, 56vh, 560px)`, three pinned cards with identical layout (text left, coral icon tile right) consume roughly three screens for a 3-item list. Time-poor scanners feel scroll effort exceed payoff, and the interchangeable coral tiles edge toward a stacked "identical card" feel.
- **Fix:** Either reduce the pin zone further (~40vh) so it reads as one quick promise, or differentiate the three step visuals with a distinct literal mini-illustration per step instead of the same gradient tile.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout` or `/impeccable delight`.

**[P2] Placeholder links leak into a finished-looking product.**
- **Why it matters:** Nav "Sign in" points to `#pricing`; every footer link (Product/Company/Support, social, Terms/Privacy/Cookies) points to `#top`. A skeptic who taps "Privacy" or "Sign in" and bounces to the top of the hero feels exactly the "something cheap" amateur signal the brand is fighting.
- **Fix:** Wire to real (even placeholder) destinations, or make non-functional links visibly non-interactive and remove "Sign in" until auth exists.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] Stats column dividers and the carousel tab/panel wiring.**
- **Why it matters:** (a) `border-left: 2px coral-soft` dividers between three symmetric stat columns are legitimate (not the banned card side-stripe) but read as a faintly templated "by the numbers" strip. (b) The carousel dots use `role="tab"`/`tablist` but the quote panels are not `role="tabpanel"` with `aria-labelledby`, so the relationship is incomplete; and auto-rotation does not pause on focus-within (a keyboard user can have content change under them).
- **Fix:** Replace the stat rules with whitespace or vary the trio's size/weight; complete the tabpanel wiring and pause rotation on focus-within.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Denise, 54, florist, on her iPhone, "been burned before":** if she sees "$0/mo" she assumes a trick and closes the tab (the P1 kill-shot for her specifically). Tapping "Privacy" and bouncing to the top reads as amateur. The blurred aurora/orbs on a mid-tier phone may stutter scroll. Wins: the 2-field form, "No payment needed yet," and "Talk to a human" are perfectly calibrated to her hesitation.

**Marcus, hardware store, scanning fast:** three pinned viewports of similar coral cards feel "long and repetitive"; he may bail before Pricing. Win: one-price Pricing with everything enumerated is exactly what a scanner wants.

**Screen-reader / keyboard user:** mostly excellent (skip link, visually-hidden marquee list, the counter exposes its true value to AT so the $0 bug does not affect SR users, focus moves to the success heading, FAQ aria wiring correct). Flags: incomplete carousel tab/tabpanel relationship; auto-rotation keeps running through focus.

## Minor Observations

- Three slightly different sticky offsets (`scroll-padding-top: 88px`, Steps `top: 96px`, FAQ `top: 100px`); harmonize for polish.

## Questions to Consider

1. Does the live Stats counter ever actually show its real value, or does the proof strip silently sit at $0 until a precise scroll position? If even 1 in 10 visitors sees "$0/mo," it is the most expensive bug on the page, and it is invisible to you because it works when you scroll slowly to it.
2. This is the animated branch: did the animation make it more trustworthy, or just more impressive? Would a calmer version with only the hero reveal and MiniSite tilt convert Denise-on-her-phone better?
3. The MiniSites are the best asset; why are they decoration in the hero and a 3-up showcase rather than the strongest interaction ("watch us build yours")?
4. Three pinned near-identical Steps cards: is "we do the hard parts" better shown in one screen than felt over three?
